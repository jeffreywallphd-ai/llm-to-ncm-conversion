# Contribution boundaries and reuse

ACL must stand alone as a complete architectural contribution with strong baselines,
fair adaptation, skip/expansion/schedule ablations and boundary analysis. Necessary
technical controls cannot be reserved for the later journal paper.

The financial journal study needs new controlled system-design evidence about evidence
handling, retrieval, calculation, validation/repair, workpapers and operating policy.
Moving the decoder to another domain or producing a financial speed table is insufficient.
Shared infrastructure/checkpoints do not constitute independent new findings.

Before journal-specific changes affect the code used for ACL, preserve an immutable
ACL source/config/checkpoint/result snapshot and archive digest. Journal research may
start before the conference decision, but overlapping manuscripts must not be under
simultaneous review. Track reused data/methods/figures/text/results explicitly. Cite
and disclose the conference contribution and substantive extension to the editor.
Folder separation or a percentage of new prose does not establish a new contribution.

Current artifacts are plans/claim maps/reports; no IS manuscript is drafted. The initial
outlet is Information Systems Frontiers, subject to the human author's final choice.
Review current guidance before submission; no universal new-material percentage is assumed.

The actual team assigns human experiment owners/reviewers and substantive student
design, implementation, analysis and writing roles. Service alone does not confer
authorship. Record work and AI assistance transparently; AI is not an author.
